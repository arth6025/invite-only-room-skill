# Invite-only room

Reverse-engineered on 17 Nov 2026 from five posts by @all.things.ftw, a Mumbai members community ("FTW", sister account @all.things.wtf). Everything they post sells one idea: **there is a room, it's worth being in, and you have to be let in.** The copy never explains the club; it withholds, teases, then hands you the door.

Out of scope: their live-footage event recaps (Barre Crawl, Recess Club) and the photo carousel Dfj-4UWn2eW.

### A — Walk reel (the strongest performer)
One man in a cap and dark sweater, rotoscoped into crosshatched illustration, steps out of a maroon door frame and walks on the spot while the world scrolls past behind him: tennis court → café with pendant lamps → park with a runner → plain wall → gallery wall of city landmarks → the maroon door slides in and he walks through it → empty olive wall → title.

| t (s) | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Steps out of the maroon door onto a textured olive wall; walk cycle starts, camera locks on him |
| 1–11 | Backgrounds scroll right-to-left, each one a place the community happens; ~2 s each |
| 11–13 | The same maroon door arrives; he walks through it |
| 13–15 | Empty olive wall, held |
| 15–18 | White bold caps: **IT ALL STARTS / IN THE ‸ ROOM**, then "right" is handwritten in above the caret |

Rule: one subject, one unbroken walk, the world changes behind him. The handwritten correction is the punchline. (The `room-reel` skill has the longer shot list and nine more ideas on this engine.)

### B — "You're not invited" twist reel
A flat illustrated anaar (cone firecracker: olive with a vermilion star and cream centre) sits over blurred real night-city footage with fireworks. A hand reaches in with a long stick and lights it. It erupts: periwinkle and vermilion flame shapes shoot up and flood the frame, then wipe to butter yellow. Text then types on, word by word:

| t (s) | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | Cone firecracker, night bokeh, distant fireworks |
| 1.5–3.8 | Hand + stick enters, fuse sparks |
| 3.8–5.8 | Eruption: flat flame shapes fill the screen, wipe to butter |

Rule: build up a reward, snatch it away, give it back to a few. Caption carries the scarcity proof ("We opened 10 spots for FTW members. They were gone in under an hour.").

### C — Drop reel (brand collab)
Flat cut-paper animation with a torn, rough-edged frame. Top-down coffee cup on periwinkle → a cream-coloured man sips from a teal Subko cup → huge wide-eyed "O" face close-up → he falls into the cup and tumbles through a maroon coffee-swirl space with vermilion and white stars → his legs disappear into the coffee → back to the top-down cup; the swirl slows; frame dims.

Rule: no product shot, no price, no CTA on screen. One sip, one reaction, one fall. The caption does the scarcity: a Hinglish two-liner ("Subko chahiye. / Subko nahi milega.": everyone wants it, not everyone gets it), then "Only for the room. Link in bio."

## Style DNA

- **Palette** (one ground colour per slide or scene):
  olive `#7E7A23` · butter `#F2EC98` · periwinkle `#8BA7F8` · charcoal `#2B2B2B` (with grain) · maroon `#5A1418` · vermilion `#F0623A` · teal `#2F9C9A` (product accents only) · ink `#141414` · white `#FFFFFF`.
  Butter on olive, butter on periwinkle and white on periwinkle are the house pairings.
- **Drawing:** loose black ink outline, a little wobbly, sparse hatching for shadow, flat fills from the palette. Figures are generic (no detailed faces, except the one reaction close-up in C). Objects are single and centred: door, keyhole, envelope, magnifier, cup, firecracker.
- **Line boil:** every static element is redrawn on 2s or 3s (8–12 fps) so the post never sits still, even on a "still" slide.
- **Type:**
  - Heavy geometric sans, all caps, for the claim. Stand-in: Urbanist ExtraBold or Sora ExtraBold.
  - **Signature effect:** a second, hand-traced outline of each letter, slightly offset and boiling, over the solid caps, in ink on light grounds and butter on dark ones.
  - Light geometric sans, sentence case or lowercase, for the thin line. Stand-in: Urbanist Light.
  - A casual monoline script for asides and sign-offs, usually underlined or trailing off. Stand-in: Homemade Apple, or Caveat if it needs to read small.
  - Giant numerals (01–04) in the same heavy sans, butter, about a quarter of the frame tall.
- **Motion:** reels are one continuous idea with physical transitions (a door, a fall, an eruption that wipes the frame), never presets. Text arrives last, word by word or line by line.
- **Sound:** must work muted. Light SFX: fuse fizz, sip, whoosh, door.

## Voice

- Withhold. "We're not telling you." "You'll have to get in to find out." Never list member benefits.
- Twist: give, take away, give back to a few ("you're not invited… okay, you are… but only some of you").
- Scarcity with a number in the caption: "10 spots. Gone in under an hour."
- Everything is "the room": "Only for the room." "See you in the room." "Happy diwali from the room."
- Hinglish is allowed and good when the brand is Indian (C).
- Caption pattern: hook line → one or two short lines → "Link in bio." / "Get your invite, link in bio."

## How to make one

1. **Lock the brief** with the user: brand, format A–E, what it announces (launch, drop, event, how-to-join), the twist or withheld answer, the scarcity number (if any), and the output size. Write the beat table (t, picture, text) before making anything.
2. **Swap the brand in** and keep the system: the brand's own ground colours may replace olive / periwinkle, but keep one ground per slide, one object, ink outline and line boil. Never use FTW's name, "WTF is FTW", or their exact lines in client work.
3. **Art:** draw or generate each object as a flat ink illustration with this prompt shell, keeping one seed and palette across the set:
   `loose hand-drawn ink outline illustration of [OBJECT], flat [FILL] fill, sparse black hatching, centred on plain [GROUND] background, no text, editorial zine style`
   For A, rotoscope one clean walk clip into crosshatch and paint the backgrounds as one long horizontal strip (see `room-reel`).
4. **Animate in HyperFrames** (load the `hyperframes` skill):
   - Line boil: three slightly different tracings of each drawing, cycled at 8–12 fps. For type, run an SVG turbulence displacement on the traced outline, re-seeded every 3 frames.
   - Reels: one continuous move, text last. Carousels: each slide is a 1–3 s seamless loop, exported as MP4.
5. **Render:** reels at 1080×1920 (A at 1440×1080 when it's a walk); carousel slides at 1080×1350. Check: readable muted, text arrives last, every slide moves, no FTW branding left over.
6. **Caption** in the voice above.

## Don'ts

- No stock-photo people, no gradients, no glossy 3D, no logo in the opening frames.
- Don't explain the product or list features. The mystery is the message.
- Don't put more than one object or one claim on a slide.
- Don't copy FTW's copy or name; this is their format, not their brand.
